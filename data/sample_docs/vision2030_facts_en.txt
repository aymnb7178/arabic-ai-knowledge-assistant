Saudi Vision 2030: General Facts

This is a short, original and general summary prepared for a demonstration. It is not an official document, and the figures should be checked against official Saudi government publications before being used anywhere.

Saudi Vision 2030 is a national strategic framework announced in April 2016. Its main aim is to reduce the dependence of the Saudi economy on oil by diversifying income sources and developing public services.

The framework is organised around three themes: a vibrant society, a thriving economy and an ambitious nation. The vibrant society theme focuses on quality of life, culture, entertainment, health and heritage. The thriving economy theme focuses on jobs, investment, small and medium enterprises and non-oil exports. The ambitious nation theme focuses on government effectiveness, transparency and responsibility.

Delivery is organised through a series of Vision Realization Programs, for example the Quality of Life Program, the Financial Sector Development Program and the National Transformation Program. Each programme has its own targets and a responsible set of government bodies.

The Public Investment Fund (PIF) is one of the main investment vehicles linked to the plan. It invests inside the Kingdom and abroad and supports the creation of new sectors and companies.

Tourism is a priority sector. The plan aims to welcome more visitors, including a higher number of Umrah pilgrims each year, and to develop destinations such as the Red Sea coast, AlUla and Diriyah. A tourist visa was introduced to make visiting the Kingdom easier.

Giga-projects such as NEOM, the Red Sea Project and Qiddiya are large development projects connected with the plan. They are designed to create new cities, destinations and job opportunities.

Increasing women's participation in the labour market is another stated goal of the plan, together with reducing unemployment and supporting Saudi talent through education and training.

Digital transformation and renewable energy also feature in the plan. The Kingdom has announced targets for generating a larger share of electricity from renewable sources.

Progress is monitored through key performance indicators, and the government has published annual progress reports for the programmes.
